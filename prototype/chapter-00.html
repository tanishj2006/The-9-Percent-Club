That is all a share is.

A share is a slice of a real business. Not a lottery ticket, not a number on a screen. When the business does well your slice is worth more. When it does badly, your slice is worth less. You carry both.

So you want your ₹2,000 back. Who buys your tokens?

You have to find a person, agree a price, and trust them to pay. That problem is the entire reason stock exchanges exist. That is Chapter 01.
